# Epic: Domain management in the app

## Descripción
Domains exist in the model (3.10.1, 8.3): `go.griddo.io` is the default, and there are tools to promote and retire one.
There's no way to see or manage them in the app.

## Requisitos
- Decide whether more than one short domain is wanted at all before building
- If so: list domains, add one (with what the ALB rule, the certificate and DNS need, documented), set the default, retire one
- The create page lets you pick the domain

## Notas
From ROADMAP 3.10.1. Low priority while go.griddo.io is the only short domain.
